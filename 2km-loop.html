Completed Choke Point

Choke Point #1: 40m Bottleneck at 15 New Temple Road

For years, this 40-metre stretch narrowed to a single lane between stone compound walls. Opposing cars could not pass, trapping traffic for kilometres. Through constructive civic dialogue with the local landholders, permission was given to pave and open the full road width.

Milestones

The Journey to a Functional Loop

From neighbourhood protests to collaborative engineering with civic bodies.

MID OCT 2026 CURRENT

BESCOM Joint Inspection & ₹17L Drive Launched

Joint site inspection conducted with BESCOM engineers for Shiva Temple junction. Technical clearance confirmed for undergrounding cables and removing 7 road poles. ₹17 Lakh fundraising launched via Whitefield Rising Trust.

SUN 4 OCT 2026 COMPLETED

40m Bottleneck Paved with Concrete

Community volunteers and engineering workers poured reinforced concrete over the 40-metre stretch on New Temple Road, funded by ₹4 Lakhs of resident contributions.

Read Times of India report ↗
SEP 2026 CONSENSUS

Landowner Agreement & Crowdfunding

Landowners generously agreed to grant public right-of-way while retaining property title, breaking a multi-year deadlock without costly litigation.

MAR 2023 TRANSIT BOOM

Nallurhalli Metro Station Opens

Namma Metro Purple Line brings thousands of daily commuters to Nallurhalli, making the 2 km loop critical for last-mile connectivity.
