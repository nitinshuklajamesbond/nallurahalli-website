Neighbourhood Directory

Nallurahalli Whereabouts

An urban village and bustling residential community tucked inside Whitefield, East Bengaluru. Situated right between tech parks, lakes, and metro stations.

🚇

Nallurhalli Metro Station

Part of the Namma Metro Purple Line connecting Whitefield to Indiranagar, MG Road, and Majestic. Station Code: VDHP. Located at the northern gateway on Whitefield Main Road.

🌿

Nallurahalli Lake

A ~24-acre historic lake with paved walking tracks and tree canopies. It serves as the primary green breathing space for morning walkers, bird watchers, and families in the neighborhood.

🏢

EPIP & ITPL Tech Corridor

Directly adjacent to Export Promotion Industrial Park (EPIP Zone), hosting global tech campuses such as SAP Labs, Tesco Campus Bengaluru, and dozens of innovation enterprises.

🏥

Vydehi Institute of Medical Sciences

A major 1,600-bed super-specialty teaching hospital and research campus located right on the edge of Nallurahalli, providing 24/7 emergency and tertiary healthcare.

🏫

Schools & Education

Home to Head Start Montessori House of Children, Glentree Academy, and nearby campuses like Gopalan National School, fostering an active young family demographic.

☕

Dining & Local Commerce

Vibrant streetscapes along Nallurahalli Main Road and New Temple Road featuring favorites like Truffles Whitefield, Paper & Pie, local darshinis, fresh markets, and artisanal bakeries.
