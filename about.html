Who We Are

About the Initiative

A community movement powered by local residents, apartment associations, and Whitefield Rising — working constructively with landowners, civic agencies, and neighbours.

Our Principles

The Nallurahalli mobility initiative was born from the conviction that community residents cannot wait indefinitely for bureaucratic processes when everyday bottlenecks stall school buses, ambulances, and thousands of commuters. By approaching issues with constructive dialogue, transparency, and pooled community funding, lasting solutions can be achieved.

Key Collaborators

  • Whitefield Rising (WFR)

    Grassroots civic collective advocating for sustainable urbanization, pedestrian rights, solid waste management, and traffic management in Bengaluru East. Key leadership on the ground provided by Zibi Jamal, Aravind Kirthi, and active volunteers.

  • Nallurahalli Residents & RWAs

    Active resident associations from Mahaveer Tranquil, Amrutha Heights, ASN Srikaram, local villager elders, small business proprietors, and educational institutions along New Temple Road.

  • Landowner Partnership

    A critical breakthrough achieved through goodwill and civic responsibility: the private owner agreed to allow road widening on their 40-metre frontage while retaining title ownership, demonstrating public-spirited generosity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 40-metre private land now government property?

No. As reported by Times of India, the legal title remains with the private owner. The owner entered into an amicable community arrangement allowing public access and road widening for community benefit.

When will the road be open to four-wheelers and two-wheelers?

Concreting was completed on Sunday, October 4, 2026. High-grade concrete requires a minimum 14-day water-ponding curing period to reach its intended load capacity. Re-opening is projected for mid-October 2026.

Will the traffic flow be converted into a one-way loop?

The community and Whitefield Rising have proposed a clockwise one-way circulation loop around New Temple Road, 2nd Main, and Siddapura Main Road to eliminate opposing gridlocks. Formal notifications and traffic policing support will follow in coordination with Whitefield Traffic Police.
