Activity Log

Project Updates & Milestones

Real-time log of ground progress, curing status, and coordination with civic authorities.

OCT 5, 2026 IN PROGRESS

Curing Phase Commences: Barricades Active

The newly concreted 40m stretch on New Temple Road has entered its critical 2-week hydration and curing phase. Water ponding and burlap covers are being maintained. The stretch remains closed to 2-wheelers and 4-wheelers to guarantee structural durability.

OCT 4, 2026 COMPLETED

Sunday Concreting Drive Executed

Scores of community members, including representatives from Whitefield Rising, nearby residential layouts (Mahaveer Tranquil, Amrutha Heights, ASN Srikaram), and local schools participated in pouring and levelling the concrete.

OCT 3, 2026 ANNOUNCEMENT

Crowdfunding Milestone Reached (₹4 Lakhs)

With over 100+ contributions from resident families and local businesses, the budget of ~₹4 Lakhs was successfully pooled to procure raw materials and masonry support.
