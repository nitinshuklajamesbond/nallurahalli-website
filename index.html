Flagship Initiative

The 2 km Loop Initiative

A 40-metre private land bottleneck narrowed New Temple Road to a single choked lane, causing peak gridlock of 30+ minutes around Nallurhalli Metro. After a year of consensus-building, residents negotiated public access, crowdfunded ₹4 Lakhs, and poured concrete on 4 Oct 2026.

Sunday 4 Oct 2026
Citizen Action

Built by Residents & Volunteers

Together with Whitefield Rising, apartment residents, school administrators, and local shop owners stepped forward on Sunday morning to lay steel reinforcement and pour concrete. The newly widened stretch is currently undergoing a 2-week curing period.

Nallurahalli Whereabouts

An urban village in the heart of East Bengaluru's Whitefield tech precinct. Connected directly by Nallurhalli Purple Line Metro, home to the 24-acre Nallurahalli Lake, and adjacent to major employment corridors at EPIP Zone and Vydehi Hospital.
